Welcome to Kigalink

Kigalink is a leading technology and management development company established in Rwanda. It combines advanced technical expertise with strategic management consulting to deliver integrated solutions. The name "Kigalink" signifies connecting clients with essential technologies and strategies, with "Kiga" referring to Kigali, Rwanda.

Our mission is to provide intelligent management and technical solutions that enable clients to achieve rapid and sustainable growth in a changing competitive environment, by combining deep local knowledge with global technical expertise.

Our Vision

To be the premier partner in Africa for entrepreneurs and companies seeking management and technical excellence, and to contribute to transforming the African continent into a global hub for digital innovation.
